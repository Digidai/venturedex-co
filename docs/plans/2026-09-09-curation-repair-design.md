# Curation repair and production rollout

Authorization: the user approved comprehensive repair/optimization and final activation after the evidence audit. This supersedes the prior local-only implementation boundary, not factual, screenshot, release, or no-duplicate-run safeguards.

## Chosen design

Keep the original rejection ledger immutable where frozen, add an evidence-bound review overlay and a fixed-pool decision manifest, and extend funding terms compatibly. Prompt-only changes cannot prevent data/validator drift. Bulk rewriting old rejections would destroy context and make a mistaken audit conclusion look like a historical fact. The selected design preserves both original decisions and subsequent corrections.

The five-company cap remains a publication ceiling, never a quota. Remove the rejection ratio from standard, task, runbook, feedback reward, validator and app prompt. Qualified overflow and operationally blocked candidates retain non-rejection states. No fresh discovery cycle or new company publication is part of this maintenance release; investigated cases enter the bounded review planner, whose outputs are not editorial acceptance.
