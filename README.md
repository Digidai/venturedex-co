# VentureDex

[VentureDex](https://venturedex.co) is a selective, source-backed startup research index. It connects public product evidence, attributed funding coverage, investor context, launch records, and explicit editorial risk notes in stable company and research pages.

## Research surfaces

- [Startup Evidence Index](https://venturedex.co/research) — current coverage, source composition, methodology, limitations, and a suggested citation
- [Editorial and source policy](https://venturedex.co/editorial-policy) — selection and evidence standards
- [Structured AI index](https://venturedex.co/ai-index.json) — typed public records and source trails
- [Full research context](https://venturedex.co/llms-full.txt) — plain-text discovery surface
- [RSS feed](https://venturedex.co/feed.xml) — recent published research

VentureDex is curated rather than exhaustive. Inclusion is not paid, and a profile is not a ranking, endorsement, investment recommendation, or estimate of the full venture market. Underlying funding facts should be checked against the original source linked from each profile.

## Citation and corrections

Use the repository's [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) metadata or the suggested citation on the [research index](https://venturedex.co/research). Cite VentureDex for its editorial synthesis and the linked original source for underlying factual claims.

Corrections should include a canonical source and can be sent to [hi@venturedex.co](mailto:hi@venturedex.co?subject=VentureDex%20research%20correction).
